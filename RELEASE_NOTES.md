# PingDrop release notes

What changed in each version of PingDrop, newest first. The Mac app is under
[Releases](https://github.com/Neerajreddyapi/Pingdrop/releases/latest) and the Android app is on
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.pingdrop).

## PingDrop 1.5

<sub>26 October 2026</sub>

- **Walk-away lock** (Pro) — your Mac locks itself when you walk away with your phone. Pick a
  distance or press **Calibrate** at your desk; it won't lock while you're typing, and unlocking
  pauses it until your phone is clearly back. Preferences › Pro › Walk-away lock.
- **Desktop widget, Siri and Shortcuts** — your phone's battery, connection and Ring on the Mac's
  desktop, and "Ring my phone with PingDrop", Stop Ringing, Phone Battery, Send Files and Open Link
  in Siri and the Shortcuts app.
- **Calls with any phone app** — allow Phone calls once (PingDrop › Settings › Calls on the phone, or
  **Allow on phone** on the Mac's call card) to answer and end calls from your Mac whatever phone app
  you use. Google's Phone app gives the most controls (mute and speaker too). Close the call card
  with × and the call stays in the menu bar with its timer; a missed call shows once.
- **Only your Mac** — the phone connects only to the Mac it was paired with, even with another
  PingDrop Mac nearby or on the same Wi-Fi.
- **Back in seconds after sleep** — open the lid and it reconnects by itself, on battery or on the
  charger; and far less battery while the Mac sleeps.
- **No pile of old notifications** when your Mac wakes — only new ones are shown.
- **Turn the webcam picture** (Pro) — ⟲ ⟳ in the PingDrop Camera window; video apps get the same turn.
- **New menu bar panel styles** — Default, Menu or Bubbles, with Liquid Glass on or off; Preferences
  is now a standard Mac Settings window with tabs.
- **"Your phone may have closed PingDrop"** — if the phone goes quiet, the Mac says why and how to fix it.
- **Send diagnostics** and a **Feature guide** in the phone's About tab.

## PingDrop 1.4.4

<sub>19 October 2026</sub>

- **Your phone gets along with other apps too** — if another app on your phone is using what
  PingDrop needs, your phone now finds another way in and your Mac follows. Messages and files from
  the Mac keep coming over Wi-Fi.

## PingDrop 1.4.3

<sub>19 October 2026</sub>

- **Your Mac gets along with other apps** — on a Mac where another app (a local web server, Docker,
  a developer tool) was using what PingDrop needs, Wi-Fi never connected and PingDrop stayed on
  Bluetooth only. It now picks a free way in by itself and tells your phone. File transfers and
  PingDrop Camera too.

---

## PingDrop 1.4.2

<sub>18 October 2026</sub>

- **Calls on your Mac** — see who's calling and answer, decline, silence, mute or end it from a
  card on your Mac, for phone calls and WhatsApp calls. The call's sound stays on your phone. Free.
- **A connection that fixes itself** — after a Wi-Fi drop, the Mac sleeping, or Bluetooth / Wi-Fi
  going off and on, PingDrop reconnects on its own. Works on your phone's hotspot and on Wi-Fi
  without internet.
- **The Mac app updates itself** — new versions install from the menu bar in one click.
- **Clearer pairing** — every step shown on both sides, and a fresh code when one expires.
- **Why it's on Bluetooth only** — both apps tell you, and a Reconnect button on the phone.
- **PingDrop Camera** (Pro) — works in more video apps and keeps going if Wi-Fi blips mid-call.
- **A fresh look** — a glass tab bar and softer buttons on the phone, Liquid Glass across the Mac app.
- **Easier on your Mac's battery** — the Mac stays asleep with the lid closed.

---

## PingDrop 1.4.1

<sub>15 October 2026</sub>

- **Your phone as a webcam** (Pro) — pick **PingDrop Camera** in FaceTime, Zoom, Meet or any
  video app on the Mac. Your phone asks once, then streams over Wi-Fi in 720p, 1080p or 2K.
- **Photo from your phone** (Pro) — click Photo on the Mac, take the shot on your phone, and it
  opens on the Mac.
- **Faster transfers** — up to 4× faster over Wi-Fi, in both directions.
- **Send a folder** — zipped and sent in one go, from the phone or the Mac.
- **Open links on the other side** — Share → Open on Mac on the phone, or Open Link in the
  Mac's menu bar.
- **Share → PingDrop** on the Mac — in Finder's Share menu and every app's Share button.
- **A small 2×1 widget** with your Mac's battery and a Ring button — free.
- **A cleaner Home screen** on the phone, and a tidier menu bar panel on the Mac.

---

## PingDrop 1.4

<sub>9 October 2026</sub>

- **Find my phone** — ring your phone from the Mac's menu bar, loudly, even on silent. And
  **Ring my Mac** from the phone, its widget or a Quick Settings tile. Pick the ring sound on
  each device.
- **Send to Phone from Finder** — right-click files → Quick Actions → Send to Phone with PingDrop.
- **Quick Settings tiles** for Send Clipboard and Ring my Mac — add them from Settings → Shortcuts.
- **Tips** on the Home screen for shortcuts you might not know about.
- On the Mac: an optional **Liquid Glass** look (macOS 26+) and a resizable Preferences window.
- **New in PingDrop Pro:** media controls on the Mac, notification history and search,
  a home-screen widget, and auto-send a folder (like Screenshots) to your Mac.
- A clearer PingDrop Pro screen.
